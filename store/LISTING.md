## Title

Nagi Feed

## Short summary

Filter X posts with your own text rules. Reveal hidden posts anytime. Jev API key required for text analysis.

## Description

Choose what you see on X with Nagi Feed.

Write your own exclusion rules in plain language. Nagi Feed checks post text and quoted text with TypeSafe AI / Jev. Confirmed text matches collapse the entire post into a compact line showing the matching rule. Click Show to reveal a post, then Collapse again when you are done.

A small 凪 mark immediately left of Grok shows whether a post is queued, being checked, checked, partially checked, uncertain, or failed. Hover for details; click to open settings. Unconfirmed matches remain visible.

Features:
- Your own text rules, with individual enable/disable controls.
- Whole-post collapse and click-to-reveal for confirmed matches.
- Official X ad-label filtering without an API key.
- Compact checking status and settings access beside Grok.
- Optional automatic “Not interested” for confirmed non-ad matches on Home. This is enabled by default, can be turned off, affects X recommendations, and depends on the action being available.

Requirements and limits:
- Text analysis requires your own TypeSafe AI / Jev account and API key. Provider charges apply according to your account's terms and usage. The extension does not include a key or analysis credits.
- Post text, quoted text, and enabled free-text rules are sent directly to TypeSafe over HTTPS using your key. The key stays in extension-local IndexedDB and is not synced by the extension. Read the privacy policy before using text analysis.
- Images and videos are not fetched or analyzed. Media-only posts remain visible. If accompanying text matches, the whole post including its media collapses.
- Nagi Feed does not translate posts. It can coexist with a separate translation extension.
- X page changes and service availability may affect operation. Text analysis can be uncertain or fail; unconfirmed matches stay visible.

Screenshots show fictional demo posts and mocked Jev responses, with the actual extension interface. Nagi Feed is an independent project and is not affiliated with X or TypeSafe.
